Our story

Local information deserves a simpler place to meet.

Local businesses often have worthwhile deals, events, job openings, and promotions, but the details can be scattered across websites, social media pages, search results, signs, and word of mouth. It can take extra effort for neighbors to know what is happening nearby.

Price Market is being built to bring those details together in one simple local marketplace. We are starting in Central Pennsylvania and building community by community, with the aim of making local discovery easier to use and easier to share.
